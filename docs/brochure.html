The three forms

Observe · Reason · Report

Like the three forms of Haki, hakigains does three things well — and nothing it shouldn't. It observes, it reasons, it reports. It never acts on your accounts.

Kenbunshoku · Observation

Observe

Pulls sleep, HRV, resting HR, Body Battery, stress and training load from Garmin, and folds indoor/outdoor variants into clean modality buckets.

ingest → readiness summary
Haoshoku · Judgement

Reason

One LLM call weighs recovery against load and your profile, then reconciles its call with Garmin's own readiness score — a second opinion, not a rubber stamp.

reason → session + rationale

Charted waters

What's aboard today

Readiness ingest

A compact daily picture from the noisy Garmin firehose — the signals that decide how ready you are to train.

Garmin second opinion

Ingests Garmin's own readiness score and reconciles — agreeing, or naming the disagreement and why.

Modality normalization

Treadmill + outdoor = "run"; indoor + road = "bike". The coach reasons over real variety, not fragments.

Gated Telegram bot

Ask it anything, grounded in today's data. Locked to your chat ID — strangers can knock, the door only opens for you.

Your profile, your knobs

Goals and constraints live in a file you own; dial intensity bias & windows live via /set.

Goal-tied new tricks

Occasionally suggests a session outside your usual pattern — but only when a stated goal clearly earns it.

The Log Pose route

How it sails

One package, two entry points. The same gated core runs the dawn cron and the live bot — so neither can skip the guard.

EventBridge (dawn)  ─►  ingest ─► reason ─► deliver → Telegram
Telegram webhook   ─►  bot/core (GATED) ─► /brief · /set · Q&A

secrets  Secrets Manager — Garmin · LLM key · bot token   (never in the repo)
state    S3 — Garmin token + settings, synced across cold starts
brain    LLM behind a provider port — Azure now, Bedrock next

Captain's code

Your Garmin credentials stay on your own ship — there is no central service holding anyone's account. hakigains observes, reasons and reports; it never sets sail with your keys. Self-host it, read every line, change the coaching in a file you own.
