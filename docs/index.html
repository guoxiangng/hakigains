Kenbunshoku · Observation

What hakigains does

Reads your sleep, HRV, resting HR, Body Battery, training load, and the 7-day trend — then forms its own read on what your body needs, and cross-checks it against Garmin's own readiness call rather than just repeating it back to you.

ingest + reason → one recommended session
Busoshoku · Armament

What you do

The recommendation means nothing until you put it into practice. Every session you actually train hardens tomorrow's read — the armor is real, not the app's to wear for you.

your training → the real hardening

Feature highlights

What it actually does

No Haki needed for these — just the engineering.

Second opinion, not a rubber stamp

Forms its own read on your readiness first, then explicitly agrees or disagrees with Garmin's own score — and says why.

Talk back to it

A gated Telegram bot: ask "why?", tune knobs live with /set, or just chat about your training. Locked to your own chat ID.

Remembers what you tell it

Say "left knee is sore, no running this week" once. It keeps that as a dated fact, uses it in tomorrow's briefing, and follows same-day follow-up questions. /memory shows what it holds; /forget deletes it.

Grows with your goals

Occasionally suggests something outside your usual pattern — but only when a stated goal clearly earns it.

Architecture

One core, two entry points

The same gated logic runs the scheduled briefing and the live bot, so neither can skip the security gate. Everything, including the model call and the coach's memory, runs inside one AWS account.

secrets  AWS Secrets Manager — Garmin creds · bot token (no LLM key to hold)
state    S3 — Garmin auth token + settings, synced across cold starts
brain    Claude Sonnet 5.5 on Amazon Bedrock, behind a provider interface
memory   Bedrock AgentCore Memory — chat turns in, dated facts out
deploy   AWS SAM · container-image Lambda · ap-southeast-1

Captain's code

Your Garmin credentials stay on your own ship — there is no central service holding anyone's account. hakigains observes, reasons, and reports; it never sets sail with your keys. Self-host it, read every line, change the coaching in a file you own.
